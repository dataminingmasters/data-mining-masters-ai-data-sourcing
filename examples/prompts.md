# Example requests

## Find a dataset supplier

Find up to three providers of licensed English and Spanish conversational audio
with transcripts for commercial speech-model training. Distinguish publicly
confirmed capabilities from inventory, pricing, or permissions that require a
provider conversation. Do not buy data or contact anyone.

## Find a licensing partner

We operate an English-language business and have internal SOPs and workflow
documentation. Find potential partners that license company data, explain their
published application requirements, and list what we must verify about our rights.
Treat our eligibility as unconfirmed until supported.

## Compare offers

Compare the supplied offers against a $10,000 total pilot budget, nonexclusive
rights, and commercial model training. Do not treat a missing fee as zero or
evaluation access as permission to train. Show exclusions and questions before
making a conditional recommendation.

## Prepare an inquiry

Create a provider-neutral data partnership brief from this aggregate inventory:
850 internally written SOP pages and 120,000 combined CRM notes and support
tickets from 2021–2026. Client-contract permissions have not been reviewed.
Use a metadata-only first step and draft an inquiry without sending it.

## Work offline

Use only the documents I provide. Compare them as dated evidence and clearly
separate what they establish from facts that need live verification. Do not
browse, invent source URLs, or claim a current market check.
