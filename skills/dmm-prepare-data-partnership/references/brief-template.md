# Data partnership brief template

## Overview

Owner/applicant: [supplied name, or To confirm]

Objective: [license an existing archive / ongoing supply / stated goal]

Prepared: [actual date]

Status: Metadata-only discussion draft. Licensing rights and transfer terms
remain subject to the unresolved items below.

## Asset inventory

| Asset | Approximate volume | Coverage | Format | Rights/handling status | Initial scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Separate confirmed facts, estimates, and unknowns. Include descriptions, not
raw records. If a volume covers several categories together, do not repeat it
as the volume of each category.

## Potential applications

State one or two plausible uses as hypotheses for the partner to assess.
Do not promise model performance, legal suitability, or revenue.

## Scope to discuss

- Intended uses and excluded uses.
- One-time or ongoing supply and update frequency if known.
- Permission, consent, contractual, confidentiality, and privacy questions.
- Commercial structure as a question or proposal, never an agreed term.

## Proposed first step

Offer a schema, aggregate inventory, or explicitly synthetic example. State
what must be confirmed before real data transfer.

## Open questions

List only issues affecting whether the next step can proceed.

## Draft inquiry

Subject: Data partnership inquiry — [asset type]

Hello [team/name to confirm],

We are assessing a potential data partnership involving [supported asset
description]. Our inventory includes [two or three supplied, non-sensitive
facts]. We would like to discuss [objective], subject to confirming the rights
and handling requirements.

Would this fit your current program? We can begin with a metadata-only inventory
or a synthetic example. Please share your accepted asset types, intended uses,
and the next evaluation step.

[Sender to confirm]

Adapt to the facts; do not leave drafting instructions in the final brief unless
the underlying value remains unknown.
